Title: Cottage Cheese Flatbread
Category: baked good
Servings: 4 flatbreads
Cook Time: 15 mins
Source: tastyhappy.com

Ingredients:
1 C cottage cheese
1 egg
1/2 t salt
1/4 t garlic powder

Instructions:
Mix all ingredients together in a bowl until well combined.
Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat.
Pour 1/4 of the batter and spread into a circle.
Cook for 3-4 minutes until golden brown.
Flip and cook other side for 2-3 minutes.
Repeat with remaining batter.

Notes: Great for meal prep! Can be frozen and reheated.

Title: Basic Pie Crust
Category: base/component
Servings: 1 double crust (9-inch pie)
Cook Time: 30 mins

Ingredients:
2.5 C flour
1 C butter
1/2 C cold water
1 t salt
1 T sugar

Instructions:
Mix flour, salt, and sugar in a large bowl.
Cut in cold butter until mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
Add water gradually, mixing until dough comes together.
Divide into two disks and wrap in plastic.
Refrigerate for at least 1 hour before rolling out.

Notes: Can be frozen for up to 3 months.

Title: Simple Tomato Sauce
Category: sauce
Servings: 4 cups
Cook Time: 45 mins
Source: Family recipe

Ingredients:
2 cans tomatoes
3 cloves garlic
2 T olive oil
1 t salt
1/2 t sugar
1/4 bunch basil

Instructions:
Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
Add minced garlic and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
Add crushed tomatoes, salt, and sugar.
Bring to a simmer and cook for 30-40 minutes, stirring occasionally.
Add torn basil leaves in the last 5 minutes.
Adjust seasoning to taste.

Notes: Great for pasta, pizza, or lasagna.

Title: Balsamic Vinaigrette
Category: dressing
Servings: 1 cup
Cook Time: 5 mins

Ingredients:
1/2 C olive oil
1/4 C balsamic vinegar
1 T honey
1 t dijon mustard
1 clove garlic
1/2 t salt
1/4 t black pepper

Instructions:
Mince garlic finely.
In a jar or bowl, combine all ingredients.
Shake or whisk vigorously until emulsified.
Taste and adjust seasoning as needed.

Notes: Store in refrigerator for up to 2 weeks. Shake before using.
