## Bacon Corn Salsa

Title: Bacon Corn Salsa
Category: appetizer
Servings: N/A
Cook Time: 5 min
Source: Image: scan_page_010.jpg

Ingredients:
1 12 oz pkg bacon, cooked and crumbles
1 bunch green onions, sliced
1 each red pepper, chopped
1 16 oz bag frozen corn, thawed under running hot water

Instructions:
1. Cook the bacon.
2. Remove all but 2 T of the bacon grease.
3. In the grease, sauté the onions and pepper for about 5 minutes.
4. Add the corn and cook until heated through.
5. Add the bacon back to the salsa.
6. Pour into a serving bowl.
7. Serve warm with tortilla chips.

## Cocktail Meat Balls

Title: Cocktail Meat Balls
Category: appetizer
Servings: N/A
Cook Time: 4 hours
Source: Image: scan_page_012.jpg

2 lbs ground beef
1 pkg onion soup mix
1 egg
0.5 C triscuits, crushed
1 14 oz bottle BBQ sauce (Catsup or chili sauce)

Instructions:
1. Spray a jelly roll pan (cookie sheet with sides) with vegetable spray.

## Penguin Appetizers

Title: Penguin Appetizers
Category: appetizer
Servings: N/A
Cook Time: N/A
Source: Image: scan_page_019.jpg

Ingredients:
18 jumbo black olives, pitted
1 (8 ounce) package cream cheese, softened
18 small black olives
1 each carrot

Instructions:
1. Cut a slit from top to bottom, lengthwise, into the side of each jumbo olive.
2. Carefully insert about 1 t of cream cheese into each olive.
3. Slice the carrot into eighteen 0.25" thick rounds.
4. Cut a small notch out of each carrot slice to form feet.
5. Save the cut out piece and press into center of small olive to form the beak.
6. If necessary, cut a small slit into each olive before inserting the beak.
7. Set a big olive, large hole side down, onto a carrot slice.
8. Set a small olive onto the large olive, adjusting so that the beak, cream cheese chest and notch in the carrot slice line up.
9. Secure with a toothpick.

Notes:
- Instead of cutting a slit to insert the beak, just cut the beaks down enough to fit into the holes of the large olives.
- It's much easier to use a knife rather than a spoon to stuff the cream cheese in the bodies. Next time try using a pastry bag.
- Regular toothpicks are too small. The large frilly picks are the perfect size if you can find them. The picks used are much larger than needed.

## Stuffed Mushrooms

Title: Stuffed Mushrooms
Category: appetizer
Servings: N/A
Cook Time: 25-30 min
Source: Image: scan_page_021.jpg

Ingredients:
30 medium white cap mushrooms
12 oz jimmy dean sausage
1 clove garlic, finely minced
0.5 C seasoned bread crumbs (or finely crushed triscuits or ritz crackers)
0.5 C parmesan cheese
1 C mozzarella cheese, grated

Instructions:
1. Spray a 9x13 casserole dish and set aside.
2. Clean the mushrooms and remove the stems.
3. Chop the stems very finely.
4. In a skillet, brown the sausage.
5. Drain all but 2 T of the grease.
6. Add the mushroom stems and garlic to the sausage and cook for 1-2 minutes.
7. Stir in the bread crumbs and parmesan cheese.
8. Stuff the mushroom caps with 1 heaping T of the sausage mixture.
9. When the casserole dish is full of mushrooms, sprinkle the mozzarella cheese over the top.
10. Bake in a 350* oven for 25-30 minutes, or until the mushrooms are tender and the cheese is melted and bubbly.

## How to Cook a Prime Rib Roast

Title: How to Cook a Prime Rib Roast
Category: main dish
Servings: N/A
Cook Time: N/A
Source: Image: scan_page_098.jpg

Ingredients:
1 each prime rib roast
salt and pepper
butter

Instructions:
1. If your rib roast is frozen, let it thaw completely in the refrigerator.
2. Remove the roast from the refrigerator 2.5 to 4 hours before cooking, the longer time for the largest roast (if you don't let the roast come to room temperature, it will take longer to cook your roast).
3. Preheat oven to 500*F.
4. Pat the rib roast dry with a paper towel or napkin.
5. Season the roast with salt and pepper.
6. Smear the cut ends of the roast with the butter.
7. Place the roast (ribs down) on the rack in the roasting pan.
8. Insert meat thermometer so tip is in thickest part of beef, not resting in fat or touching bone.
9. Sear the rib roast for 15 minutes at the higher oven temperature (500*F).
10. Turn the oven to the lower temperature (325*F) for the rest of the cooking time.
11. Every 0.5 hour, baste the cut ends of the roast with the fat accumulated in the roasting pan.
12. You cook the prime rib roast approximately 14-17 minutes per pound.
13. About 0.5 hour before the estimated end of the roasting time, begin checking the internal temperature.
14. Cook until rib roast reaches an internal temperature of 120*F.
15. Remove from oven, cover with aluminum foil, and let sit approximately 20 to 30 minutes.

Notes: Remember, the roast will continue to cook as it sets. The temperature will rise to 125 to 130*F internal temperature (medium rare). To hold cooked roast until serving time, immediately turn off oven and leave door ajar after removing roast. Let roast sit 15 minutes on counter and then return roast to the oven, door closed, for up to an hour or even 2 hours for the biggest roasts. Check the temperature every 15 minutes. It will rise approximately 10*F at first, then gradually subside.

## Thin Crust Pizza Dough

Title: Thin Crust Pizza Dough
Category: main dish
Servings: N/A
Cook Time: N/A
Source: Image: scan_page_110.jpg

Ingredients:
1 lb (or about 3.5 C) high gluten flour
0.75 C warm water
1 T vegetable oil
1.5 t active dry yeast
1.5 t sugar
1 t salt

Instructions:
1. In a heavy-duty stand mixer (e.g., Kitchen Aid) fitted with dough hook, add the water, oil, yeast, salt, and sugar.
2. Mix thoroughly until yeast has fully dissolved.
3. Add flour and mix on low speed until all of the flour and water have mixed and a stiff dough ball forms, about 3 to 4 minutes.
4. Stop mixing as soon as the dough ball forms as this type of dough should not be kneaded.
5. Place the dough ball into a large bowl and cover tightly with plastic wrap.
6. Let the dough rise for 24 hours in the refrigerator before using.

Notes: Please note that I cannot overemphasize the importance of a 24-hour rising time since it is absolutely essential for the dough to develop its signature texture and, more importantly, its unique flavor! Do not skip this step!

7. Preheat your oven to 500*F about one hour before you plan to bake the pizza.
8. Turn the dough out onto a large surface and dust with flour.
9. Using a heavy rolling pin (or dough sheeter), roll the dough out very thin to form a 24-inch or larger circle.
10. If you're using a cutter pizza pan (recommended), dust the pan lightly with flour, place the dough in the pan and dock.
11. Use the rolling pin to trim off the excess dough drooping over the sides of the pan.
12. If you wish to cook the pizza directly on a pizza stone (not using a pan), then place the dough on a dusted pizza-peel, dock, and fold the edge over 1-inch all the way around and pinch it up to form a raised lip or rim.
13. Optionally, precook the crust for 4 minutes before adding any sauce or toppings.
14. Remove the crust from the oven and pop any large air pockets that may have formed.
15. Add the sauce, shredded mozzarella cheese, and your favorite toppings.
16. Continue baking, on the lowest oven rack, rotating the pan half way through so that it cooks evenly, until crust is sufficiently browned and crisp, about 10 to 15 minutes.
17. Remove the pizza from the oven and slide pizza out of cooking pan onto a large wire cooling rack or cutting board.
18. Allow to cool for 5 minutes before transferring to a serving pan.

Notes: This step allows the crust to stay crisp while it cools, otherwise the trapped steam will soften the crust.

## Gravy

Title: Gravy
Category: side dish
Servings: N/A
Cook Time: N/A
Source: Image: scan_page_121.jpg

Ingredients:
2 C juices from cooked meat + enough broth to equal 2 C
2 T corn starch (or 2 T flour)
0.5 C cold broth (or 0.5 C milk if using flour)
salt and pepper to taste
1 t browning liquid (for beef gravy)

Instructions:
1. Collect whatever juices you can get off your cooked meat + enough broth to equal 2 C.
2. Try to get off as much fat from the juices as you can.
3. Put the juices/broth in a skillet, and bring to a boil.
4. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
5. Once the juices are boiling, mix 0.5 C cold broth with 2 T corn starch (or 0.5 C milk with 2 T flour), and slowly add to the pan.
6. Stir constantly, so you won't get lumps.
7. With beef gravy, you should add 1 t browning liquid (this can be found in any grocery store).
8. Continue to cook the gravy for 5 minutes on medium lo heat, stirring occasionally.

Instructions:
